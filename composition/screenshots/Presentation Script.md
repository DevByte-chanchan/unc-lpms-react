# LPMS Learning Plan Composition: Presentation Script (by user)

About 10 minutes. **A** talks, **B** drives the screen. One browser window per role, already signed in. Sessions last 8 hours, so sign in again on the day. Demo accounts all use `demo1234` (instructor, Program Head, Director of Libraries, Industry Consultant, Dean, VPAA).

Order of the demo follows the real flow: set-up by the Program Head, writing by the Instructor, review, decision, then the read-only roles.

---

## 0. Opening (45 seconds) — A
"Our module is Learning Plan Composition. A learning plan is the course syllabus. Each role has its own pages and permissions, and one syllabus moves through one approval chain: **Program Head and Director of Libraries review it at the same time, then the Dean decides.** The Industry Consultant comments and sits outside the chain."

---

## 1. Program Head: set-up (2 minutes)
Sidebar: Learning Plan, PO & PEO Alignment, CO & PO Alignment, COAEP.

1. **PO & PEO Alignment.** Show which Program Outcomes map to which Program Educational Objectives. Say: "These are stored in the database and feed the syllabus pages."
2. **CO & PO Alignment.** Show the Course Outcomes mapped to Program Outcomes for a course.
3. **COAEP.** Open the Course Outcomes Assessment and Evaluation Plan, and show generate and export.
4. **Learning Plan.** Show the course table. Say: "The Program Head assigns the course and lead instructor for the term, and assigns an Industry Consultant to the course."
5. **Poke.** Show the poke on an instructor who has **not yet submitted**. Say: "A reminder to submit, not a review request."

---

## 2. Instructor: write and submit (2 minutes)
Sidebar: Learning Plan, Profile.

1. **Profile.** Show the e-signature setup. Say: "The signature goes on the syllabus."
2. **Learning Plan.** Open the assigned course offerings, then open the syllabus.
3. Walk the sections: course details, ILOs, topics, the teaching and learning activities, grading criteria, the program alignment pages, and references (picked from the library).
4. **Preview.** Open the syllabus preview and show the PDF.
5. **Submit.** Say: "After submitting, editing is locked until a reviewer returns it."
6. **Notification bell.** Show a notification, for example a return with comments.

---

## 3. Director of Libraries: references and review (1.5 minutes)
Sidebar: Learning Plan (reviews), Reference Library.

1. **Reference Library.** Add a reference, then view or edit one. Say: "Instructors pick from this library, so references are consistent."
2. **Learning Plan.** Open the submitted syllabus, read the references section, add a comment, and accept or return it.

---

## 4. Program Head: review (1 minute)
1. Learning Plan, then the approval course table.
2. Open the same submission, read it, comment, and accept.
3. Say: "Each reviewer sees only their own comments. If either reviewer returns it, it goes to the instructor. After a resubmit, only the reviewer who returned it reviews again; the other acceptance still counts."

---

## 5. Industry Consultant (45 seconds)
1. Learning Plan, then open the syllabus.
2. Add a comment. Say: "The consultant gives input but does not approve."

---

## 6. Dean: decision (1.5 minutes)
Sidebar: Learning Plan, Faculty, Programs, form settings.

1. Open the plan. Say: "It reaches the Dean only after both reviewers accept."
2. Approve it, or return it with comments to the instructor.
3. **Form settings.** Show editing the ISO form revision and effectivity date.
4. Show the Faculty and Programs overview pages.

---

## 7. VPAA (30 seconds)
1. **Approved Plans.** Show the list, with the year and semester filter.
2. Open one and export. Say: "The VPAA sees only approved plans, read-only."

---

## 8. Superuser (30 seconds, optional)
1. **User Management.** Create an account, change a role, or deactivate a user.
2. Show the audit trail. Say: "Every sign-in, submission and decision is recorded."

---

## Closing (20 seconds) — A
"LPMS gives each learning plan one clear path, and every role sees only what it needs. Thank you. We welcome your questions."

---

## Questions you may get

**Not built yet** (say it plainly; do not demo these):
- Upload pages for the alignment and COAEP documents (alignment is entered and stored in the system instead).
- A document repository.
- The Program Head forwarding the Dean's comments. Today the Dean returns directly to the instructor.
- A Program Head endorse button. Today the plan goes to the Dean automatically after both reviewers accept.
- VPAA search beyond the year and semester filter.
- Industry Consultant approval. Today the consultant comments only.
- The Dean's vision, mission and indicators (still being confirmed by the group).

**Other answers**
- *Real data?* Demo data on a local setup.
- *Why do two reviewers go in parallel?* To shorten the approval time. The Dean sees the plan only after both accept.
- *Passwords?* Stored as hashes. Sessions last 8 hours and are renewed at every sign-in.
- *Privacy?* There is a Privacy Notice page with the Data Protection Officer's contact, and registration records the accepted version and the time.

- Data looks empty: say "demo database", and switch to the screenshots in `Downloads\Screenshots\<role>`.
